# Policy evaluation

Generated by `evaluation/harness.py --matrix` against the running platform and the official simulator image. The simulator is deterministic, so re-running gives the same numbers. Every run starts from a fresh world; `do_nothing` is the same world with nobody acting (advisory mode, nothing approved).

## Summary

- **baseline, 3 simulated days:** demand served **30.7 % → 100.0 %** with the platform; unmet fuel 193,586 L → 0 L.
- **crisis, 3 simulated days:** demand served **25.6 % → 99.2 %** with the platform; unmet fuel 249,805 L → 2,647 L.
- **single route cut, 3 simulated days:** demand served **28.8 % → 100.0 %** with the platform; unmet fuel 212,597 L → 0 L.
- **Across all 10 platform runs: 0 failed shipments and 0 litres lost to overflow.** Every shipment passes the same constraint checks, whichever policy proposed it.
- **When fuel truly runs out (6 days), the optimizer protects the worst-served station** at about the same total: worst station heuristic → optimizer: baseline 0.87 → 0.92; crisis 0.72 → 0.73.

## How to read this

- **Scenarios:** `baseline` = normal operations, no injected events; `crisis` = demand ×1.6 everywhere for 24 h, the next supply arrival halved, two routes cut for 12 h, a supply delay of 6 h and a 4 h station outage, overlapping; `single_route_cut` = the only routes to Tongi and Cox's Bazar cut for 10 h during a ×1.4 demand spike.
- **600-tick rows are a stress test of scarcity, not of the planner.** The scheduled supply arrivals end at tick 212, so after day 3 there is physically not enough fuel for everyone and no policy can reach 100 %. What matters there is that nothing is wasted (0 failed, 0 overflow) and how fairly the shortage is shared (worst station).
- **Heuristic vs optimizer.** With enough fuel both serve ~100 %; the greedy heuristic is sometimes a hair ahead because the optimizer plans conservatively against forecast uncertainty. The optimizer earns its keep under scarcity (fairness above) and with coupled limits (one depot's dispatch capacity is shared by all three fuels). The heuristic is also its automatic fallback.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Service level | litres served / litres demanded, from the simulator's own counters |
| Unmet L | demanded but not served (a station was dry or closed) |
| Worst station SL | service level of the worst-served station (fairness) |
| Failed shipments | shipments the simulator marked FAILED (fuel lost on a disrupted route) |
| Overflow L | fuel that arrived at a full station and was lost |
| Depot clipped L | supply that arrived at a full depot and was lost because nobody moved fuel out |
| Shipped L | litres dispatched by the platform |
| Decide p95 ms | 95th-percentile time to produce a plan |
